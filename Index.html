How to Use

  1. Upload any photo of a room, city, landscape, or portrait
  2. Click "Convert to Sketch" to transform it into two versions
  3. View the full sketch with shading at the top
  4. Adjust the intensity slider to control the sketch detail level for both versions
  5. Enable the eraser tool to remove unwanted portions from either sketch
  6. View the outline-only version (no shading) at the bottom
  7. Click the Download button on each sketch to save that version separately
